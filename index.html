Traffic Jam support

Tap the cars out of the jam, park them in the bays and fill every seat before the bays run out.

Common questions

  • I'm stuck on a level. Every level is checked to be solvable. Tap Hint (it's free) to see a car that helps the front of the line, or use a booster such as Extra Bay or U-Turn.
  • My progress disappeared. Progress is saved only on your phone, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone starts the game again.
  • How do I start over? Settings › Reset Progress, then tap it a second time to confirm.
  • Does it need the internet? No. The game works fully offline.
