Traffic Jam: Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026 · Applies to Traffic Jam on iOS and Android (app ID com.sigmatack.trafficjam)

Traffic Jam is a puzzle game made by Sigmatack ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: we don't collect any.

Information we collect

None. Traffic Jam has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game doesn't send anything about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, Traffic Jam saves a small amount of data on your phone:

  • the levels you have unlocked and the level you are on,
  • your coin balance and boosters,
  • your win streak, daily reward and daily puzzle progress,
  • your sound, vibration and reminder settings, and whether you've seen How to Play.

This data stays on your device. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it, and Settings › Reset Progress inside the game clears it at any time.

Permissions

After your first win the game may ask to send notifications. If you allow it, it schedules up to three reminders on your phone (for example, that today's daily puzzle is ready), never more than one a day. These reminders are created on your device; no notification service or server is involved, and nothing about you is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

The game also plays sound effects and can vibrate, neither of which needs your approval. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Third parties

The game includes no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. If you download it from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google may collect information under their own privacy policies; that is outside the game's control.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game starts handling data differently, for example by adding ads, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
